Gaurav Najpande

Visiting Researcher · Arizona State University

I am a visiting researcher at the Arizona State University CoRAL Lab, working with Prof. Vivek Gupta. I earned my M.S. in Data Science, Analytics and Engineering from ASU in 2026.

My research is centered on trustworthy AI: helping models reason reliably over structured and multimodal data, and ensuring that their answers are supported by verifiable evidence. I work on table understanding, diagram reasoning, evaluation, and retrieval.

I also enjoy building research systems end to end — from datasets and experiments to usable applications.
