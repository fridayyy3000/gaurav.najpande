Gaurav Najpande

Trustworthy AI · NLP · Multimodal Reasoning

I am a visiting researcher at Arizona State University, working with Prof. Vivek Gupta at the CoRAL Lab. I completed my M.S. in Data Science, Analytics and Engineering at ASU in 2026.

My work focuses on making AI systems more reliable when reasoning over tables, diagrams, and complex documents — especially how they transform information, identify supporting evidence, and resolve conflicting sources.

I'm a tinkerer at heart: I enjoy turning research questions into practical systems and rigorous evaluations.
