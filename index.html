Gaurav Najpande

Visiting Researcher · CoRAL Lab · Arizona State University

I am a visiting researcher at Arizona State University, working with Prof. Vivek Gupta in the CoRAL Lab. I received my M.S. in Data Science, Analytics and Engineering from ASU in 2026.

My research focuses on trustworthy language and multimodal AI, particularly how language and vision-language models understand and reason over structured data, visual information, and complex documents. I study how these systems represent information, identify relevant context, and ground their predictions in the right evidence.

I am especially interested in multimodal reasoning, evidence attribution, retrieval, and evaluation. My goal is to develop AI systems that can handle complex, evolving, or conflicting information while producing accurate, efficient, and verifiable outputs that are traceable to their sources.

Research directions

My current work centers on structured and multimodal reasoning, evidence attribution and source reliability, and evaluation of language and vision models. I am particularly interested in building systems that can identify the evidence behind a claim, rather than only produce plausible answers.
